    Before you compete

    Membership is required.

    A current UPSA membership is required for athletes entering UPSA-sanctioned Pilipinas Surfing National Tour events. Review the current rules and select the plan that suits your competition schedule.

    Read the UPSA Rulebook ↗
    Two ways to join

    Choose your plan

    Both plans provide the membership required to compete; access and validity depend on the option selected.

    01 / Full seasonBest for multiple events

    Annual Membership

    One membership covers the Pilipinas Surfing National Tour legs throughout the competition season. It remains valid until the season officially closes.

    ValidityOne season
    Event accessAll national tour events

    PHP 1,000per season

    Choose annual membership ↘
    02 / Single eventOne competition

    One-Time Membership

    A single-event membership applies to one event only. It cannot be carried forward to the next event you register for.

    ValidityOne event
    Event accessOne event only

    PHP 500per event

    Choose one-time membership ↘

    Membership plans, eligibility, and event requirements may be subject to the latest UPSA Rulebook and registration instructions.

    Renew your membership

    1. Find your Athlete ID in the 2025 Pilipinas Surfing National Tour rankings ↗.

    Continue to renewal form ↗
    Step 2 / New member

    Register for UPSA membership

    1. Complete the new-member registration form with accurate information.
    2. Choose and pay for your membership plan.
    3. Register for the events you want to enter.
    Continue to registration form ↗

    The Google Forms are managed by UPSA. After UPSA connects each response sheet, responses are synced to its private admin list for application review. Never submit card or bank account numbers, security codes, passwords, or bank login details.
